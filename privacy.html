Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Hermes Work Copilot is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): upcoming events, to send the owner private reminders. It never creates, changes or deletes events.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): only files the app itself created — the owner's encrypted backups. It cannot see any other Drive files.

What it does with the data

Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
